# Plantty Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: [Insert Date]_

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Plantty collects, stores, and how that data is used.

---

### 1. What We Collect
- Your Discord user ID
- Message content (for command interactions only — not full message logs)
- Server settings (to customize bot behavior)

### 2. Why We Collect It
- To enable features like personalized commands, moderation, or memory-based responses
- To improve bot functionality

### 3. What We Don’t Collect
- We don’t track your IP address, real name, location, or any personally identifiable information beyond what Discord already provides.
- We don’t share or sell your data.

### 4. Data Retention
Data is stored as long as necessary for bot operation and can be deleted on request by a server admin.

### 5. Your Rights
- You can request that we delete data related to your user ID.
- Server owners can reset bot settings anytime.

---

For concerns, email us at darknebai@gmail.com or message a Plantty developer in your server.
